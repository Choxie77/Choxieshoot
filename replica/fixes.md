## 3. What is unsolved (positioning)

Privacy-sensitive Mac users (support, finance, health, legal) want Lightshot's
fast “capture → annotate → copy” loop but cannot use a cloud-first tool for
screenshots that contain customer data. No reviewed tool pairs that loop with
local-only processing and on-device text extraction.

Non-goals from the reviews: Windows/Linux ports and HDR capture (platform and
colour-pipeline work, not a macOS capture clone); cloud gallery/upload.

## 5. Positioning options

1. **For privacy-sensitive Mac users who would use Lightshot but cannot upload
   their screens, Choxieshoot keeps every capture local and still copies the
   annotated image or its text in one keystroke.** Evidence: privacy (2 reviews,
   1 source) + capture workflow (3 reviews, 1 source). *Recommended.*
2. For people who take screenshots all day, Choxieshoot is the annotate-and-copy
   loop without an account. Evidence: capture-workflow theme.
3. For Mac users tired of cloud screenshot tools, Choxieshoot is a small native
   menu-bar utility that never phones home. Evidence: privacy theme.

**Caveat:** one source and 15 comments is a direction, not validation. Collect
App Store, G2/Capterra and Reddit data before pricing or launch claims.
